Microprocessor Architecture Visualizer for Interactive Learning, Execution, and Runtime Analysis

MAVILERA is an open-source educational platform for exploring microprocessor architecture through interactive simulation, visualization, and instruction execution. It runs entirely in your browser: no account, no installation, and no program or data is uploaded.
